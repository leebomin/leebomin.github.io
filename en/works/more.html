P07 · Short/Multivariate Statistics · solo · SAS · 2024.09–12

Definition Bias

Multivariate analysis of the local extinction index

Finding

Ran regression, PCA, factor analysis, LDA and MANOVA on 229 districts and 9 variables. Cutting variables from 9 to 5 kept adj R² at 0.857, and four components explained 85.3%.

Limits

The elderly-alone rate and the response share a denominator (age 65+), so much of the −0.908 correlation may come from the formula itself.

P08 · Short/Regression Analysis I · solo · SAS · 2024.03–06

Influence

Regression diagnostics for vacant-house rates

Finding

All-subsets and stepwise selection chose the same 4 variables, adj R² 0.587. Dropping 12 influential points moved it to 0.633, which I reported as a measure of instability, not a better model.

Limits

The old-housing coefficient has the opposite sign from its simple correlation (+0.33), due to information shared with other variables, so I did not read it causally.
