A/B Testing

Lodging app conversion A/B test · rule fixed in advance

GitHub
Period
2026.10
Team
TOBIG'S DA track · individual assignment
Role
Experiment design · decision rule · full analysis
Method
User-level assignment · delta method · SRM test · MDE

The analytics team had diagnosed a gap of about 10.7%p in list → detail conversion for the promotion channel. I tested a fix (showing the discounted price on list cards) and designed and judged the experiment by rules set before seeing the results.

What I did

  1. Assigned by user and corrected the variance with the delta method. With a design effect of 1.59 → 2.11, set MDE 3%p and a 21-day duration.
  2. Checked the split (SRM p = 0.71, 3,446 vs 3,415 users) and split the analysis around a day-8 ad campaign, treating the post-ad period as exploratory only.
  3. List → detail conversion +2.75%p (95% CI 0.96–4.54), no significant guardrail harm → recommended a staged rollout.

Key findingPeeking

Situation

On the same data I compared a fixed 21-day decision with checking daily and stopping.

Doubt

Watching p-values daily can end the test on a lucky low day, or drop a launch even when the effect is real.

Check

Stopping on day 12 gave user-level p = 0.084, meaning no launch; the fixed 21-day decision gave p = 0.0026, +2.75%p.

Of the 10.7%p observed gap, the experiment confirmed 2.75%p. I did not pin the observed gap on a single cause, and fixed the decision rule and unit of analysis before seeing results.

Takeaway

Decision rules come before results
